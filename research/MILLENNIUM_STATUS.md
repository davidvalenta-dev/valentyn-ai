# Millennium research boundary — 2026-11-10

Valentyn has not solved a Millennium Prize Problem. Its current propositional language cannot express these problems. A useful path begins with formalizing existing elementary results, not claiming that synthetic proof scores imply research capability.

The [Clay Mathematics Institute's official hub](https://www.claymath.org/millennium-problems/) lists Poincaré as solved, Navier–Stokes under Active, and the other five under Unsolved at the time of inspection. A claim, external review and acceptance of a prize solution are distinct events. The [OpenAI Navier–Stokes announcement](https://openai.com/index/navier-stokes-solution/) concerns forced singularities; its relationship to Clay's precise problem requires mathematical assessment. The [October community review announcement](https://openai.com/index/building-scientific-understanding-with-academic-communities/) discusses Yang–Mills existence; existence alone is not the full mass-gap problem. The linked proof portal was inaccessible during this audit, so no independent artifact verification is claimed.

| Direction | Required foundations | First bounded study target |
|---|---|---|
| P versus NP | complexity, reductions, circuit lower bounds | formalize elementary reductions |
| Riemann hypothesis | complex analysis, analytic number theory | verified finite identities and known estimates |
| Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer | algebraic geometry, elliptic curves, L-functions | elementary elliptic-curve group facts |
| Hodge conjecture | topology, complex algebraic geometry, cohomology | existing low-dimensional examples |
| Navier–Stokes | PDE, functional analysis, fluid mechanics | known energy inequalities with explicit hypotheses |
| Yang–Mills and mass gap | gauge theory, constructive QFT, analysis | precise definitions and existing finite-dimensional results |

These targets are proposals, not implemented Lean developments. Future claims require a faithful formal statement, independently checked dependencies, expert interpretation and novelty assessment. No announcement has been counted as a Valentyn result.
